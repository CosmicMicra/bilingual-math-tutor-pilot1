Welcome

This session takes about 15–20 minutes.

It’s part of a research study on how people pick up language when solving math problems. You don’t need to know Spanish — we’re running this with English speakers.

There are 5 short parts:

  • Baseline — A few simple math questions in English (no hints). This checks your math level before we add Spanish.
  • Practice — One Spanish question with optional English hints. Not scored — just so you can try the hint buttons.
  • Training — More Spanish questions with the same optional hints (simpler Spanish → word help → full English if you need it). Use hints only if you’re stuck.
  • Check-in — Spanish questions again, but no hints. This shows how much the hints helped and whether you can solve them on your own.
  • Word match — A short quiz: match a few Spanish math words (like más, cuántos) to their English meanings.

At the end, download your results file and send it to the researcher.
